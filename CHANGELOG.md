# Unwind changelog

This one file feeds three places, so nothing is written twice: the What's New pop-up in Preferences > Updates
(the build copies it into the app), the notes on each GitHub release, and the Updates page. Add a new
`## vX.Y` section at the top for every version. Lines starting with `###` become small headings.

## v2.5

### Hold OK on an app
- Hold OK on any app in the home row and a small menu opens: App info, Move, Remove from Home and Uninstall.
- App info opens Android's own page for that app. Uninstall hands it to Android's uninstall screen (apps built into the TV can't be removed, and the menu says so).
- Move picks the app up. Left and Right carry it along the row (Up and Down too in Stacked rows), OK puts it down, Back cancels. The new order is saved and goes into your backup.

### Home widgets
- New Preferences tab, Home widget. Pick what sits above your apps.
- The greeting is now the Unwind Greetings widget. Editing the words works the same, it just lives in the new tab.
- Month calendar: this month with today marked by a see-through shape that follows your icon shape.
- Clock: digital or analog. While it is on, the small clock in the top bar hides and the bar closes up.
- Weather and Date widgets, or Nothing for a clean, minimal home.
- Every widget is see-through. No panels, just thin type on your wallpaper.

### One place for apps
- Search now lives at the top of the All apps page (and the Favorites picker). Press OK on the bar to type with the TV's own keyboard; the list filters as you type. In folder view, typing searches every folder at once.
- The separate Search page and its dock button are gone, so the dock has three buttons: Home, Apps, Preferences.
- The search button in the top-right bar is gone too. The bar now starts with the gear.

### Cleaner Clear style
- Clear panels no longer have a bright outline or the ring-like edge band. They just end where the glass ends. The focus ring still shows on whatever you have selected.

### Smoother app opening
- Home zooms in a little and fades as an app opens, then eases back when you return. One switch in Sound & motion turns it off.

## v2.4

### Faster and lighter
- Panels, pop-ups and Preferences now appear right away. The Clear style is drawn by the graphics chip instead of being calculated pixel by pixel, so there is no waiting the first time you see it.
- The welcome screen opens without a pause.
- Fewer redraws across the launcher, so moving around with the remote feels more immediate.

### Remote control
- Preferences works completely with the D-pad. Up and Down move between settings, Left and Right move along a row, and the page scrolls to follow the highlight.
- OK always acts on the card that is highlighted.
- Coming back from the greeting editor or the picture list puts the highlight back where you left it.

### Updates
- The Updates page now shows the version installed on this TV next to the newest release on GitHub, each from its own source.
- New What's New button shows what changed in the version you have installed.
- In-app updater: checks GitHub, downloads and installs new versions.

### Look
- The three panel styles are now called Clear, Tinted and Frosted.

### Also in this version
- Backup and restore your whole setup as one file.
- Nature-sound move sounds (water drop, wind chime, bamboo, songbird, pebble) with three volumes.
- Bouncy motion, one switch to turn it off.
- Home setup tab with a step-by-step guide for setting Unwind as your Home screen from a computer.
